How to Play

Welcome, Adventurer!

The Lich's Tower is an interactive text adventure. You will explore a mysterious tower, face dangerous creatures, and make decisions that change the story.

Read each scene carefully and choose what your character should do next. There are different paths to explore and multiple endings to discover.

Create Your Character

Before entering the tower, you will choose a name for your hero and select two starting items. Each item provides different opportunities during your adventure.

Choose carefully! You will not be able to carry all three items at the beginning of the game.

Your Equipment

Holy Sword

A blessed weapon capable of fighting dark creatures. It may help you survive dangerous encounters.

Invisibility Potion

A magical potion that allows you to become invisible for a short time. It can help you avoid enemies, but it can only be used once.

Enchantment

A magical spell that can help you overcome certain obstacles and protect yourself from danger.

Choices and Consequences

Your decisions matter. Some actions require specific equipment, while others depend on what you discovered earlier in the story.

Your equipment may be used, lost, or changed during the adventure. Sometimes, words can be more powerful than weapons.

Saving Your Progress

Your adventure will be saved in your browser. You can visit the Chronicle page to review your progress and continue your journey later.

Your saved progress is stored on your current browser and device.
